Add `gen_ai.invoke_workflow.inference_calls` and `gen_ai.invoke_workflow.tool_calls` metrics to track how many inference (model) and tool calls are made during a single GenAI workflow execution. Calls made in a nested workflow are also recorded against every enclosing workflow execution, while `gen_ai.invoke_agent.inference_calls` and `gen_ai.invoke_agent.tool_calls` count each call once.
