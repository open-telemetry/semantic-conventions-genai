Mark the `mcp.method.name` well-known values `logging/setLevel`, `roots/list`, `notifications/roots/list_changed`, and `sampling/createMessage` as deprecated per [SEP-2577](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/seps/2577-deprecate-roots-sampling-and-logging) (MCP 2026-07-28 revision, status Final).
