Add `gen_ai.usage.cost.amount`, `gen_ai.usage.cost.currency`, and `gen_ai.usage.cost.source` span attributes and `gen_ai.client.operation.cost` histogram metric for per-operation cost tracking. `cost.source` applies a receive-vs-derive test: `provider` when the value was present in the response the client received (from the model provider or a priced intermediary), `local` when the emitting component computed it at recording time from pricing data it owns. The recorded amount is frozen at recording time; backend or collector-side enrichment is out of scope for `gen_ai.usage.cost.*`.
